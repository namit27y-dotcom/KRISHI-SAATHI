## 📌 About

**Krishi Saathi** is a digital agriculture marketplace designed to make agricultural products easier to discover, compare, and purchase.

The platform brings essential farming products such as **seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, tractors, water pumps, sprayers, irrigation equipment, and farm tools** into one centralized marketplace.

The project focuses on providing a **simple, responsive, and user-friendly digital experience** for farmers, agricultural buyers, retailers, and suppliers.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties while finding and purchasing agricultural products.

### Common Challenges

- Limited product availability
- Difficulty finding suitable products
- Difficulty comparing prices
- Dependence on local suppliers
- Lack of centralized product information
- Difficulty discovering specialized farming equipment
- Limited access to digital agricultural marketplaces

---

## 💡 Solution

Krishi Saathi provides a centralized marketplace where users can explore agricultural products and manage their purchases digitally.

### The platform allows users to:

- 🔎 Search for agricultural products
- 🗂️ Browse products by category
- 📦 View product information
- 💰 Check product prices
- ⭐ View product ratings
- 🛒 Add products to cart
- 📱 Access the platform on different devices
- 🏪 Explore products from agricultural suppliers

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔎 Product Search

Users can search for agricultural products using keywords, making it easier to find specific products.

### 🗂️ Category Filtering

Products are organized into different agricultural categories so users can quickly find what they need.

### 📦 Product Details

Each product can contain:

- Product name
- Category
- Price
- Description
- Supplier
- Rating
- Availability
- Product image

### 🛒 Shopping Cart

Users can add products to their cart and manage their selected products before checkout.

### ⭐ Product Ratings

Product ratings provide users with additional information when exploring products.

### 💰 Product Pricing

Products display their prices clearly, allowing users to compare available options.

### 📱 Responsive Design

The application is designed to provide a consistent experience across:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile
