# Krishi Saathi (कृषि साथी)

> **AI-Powered Smart Agriculture & Livestock Farming Platform**  
> Empowering farmers, livestock keepers, agri-entrepreneurs, and buyers with botanist-grade crop diagnosis, multimodal livestock academies, hyper-local weather advisory, government scheme matching, and a direct farm-to-buyer marketplace in regional Indian languages.

---

## 1. Project Overview

**Krishi Saathi** is an end-to-end full-stack digital agriculture ecosystem built to bridge the technology divide for smallholder farmers, dairy producers, and agri-entrepreneurs across India. Developed with modern web technologies, the platform combines multimodal Generative AI (Google Gemini), GPS-based agronomy, and responsive offline-first accessibility to deliver timely, actionable advice directly to the field.

Unlike single-purpose farming apps or generic e-commerce portals, Krishi Saathi addresses the entire agricultural lifecycle:
- **Agronomy & Plant Health**: Real-time camera-based crop disease diagnosis, botanist-grade plant taxonomy, soil compatibility checks, and tailored fertilizer/irrigation planners.
- **Animal & Aquatic Husbandry**: A dedicated 8-discipline farming academy covering dairy cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, pigs, apiculture (beekeeping), aquaculture (fisheries), and integrated circular farming.
- **Agricultural Marketplace**: Direct crop listing, agricultural input purchasing (seeds, organic composts, machinery, irrigation gear), in-app buyer-seller chat negotiation, and order tracking.
- **Localized Accessibility**: A bilingual UI displaying English alongside regional Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Maithili, Bhojpuri, Punjabi, Bengali, Telugu, and Tamil) with integrated text-to-speech and audio transcription.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Indian agriculture supports more than 50% of the workforce, yet smallholder and marginal farmers face systemic disadvantages:

1. **Information Asymmetry & Language Barriers**: Extension services and technical advisories are rarely available in farmers' mother tongues or regional dialects (such as Maithili, Bhojpuri, or rural Marathi).
2. **Crop Loss from Delayed Disease Identification**: Pests and fungal infections (e.g., blight, rust, powdery mildew) frequently destroy 20–40% of yields before an agricultural expert can visit the farm.
3. **Fragmented Livestock Knowledge**: Small ruminant (sheep/goat) and poultry farmers lack structured, step-by-step guidance on breeding calendars, biosecurity, disease vaccination schedules, and feed conversion optimization.
4. **Middlemen & Exploitative Pricing**: Farmers capture only a fraction of market value due to layered intermediaries, lack of price discovery, and minimal bargaining power.
5. **Climate Volatility & Input Mismanagement**: Erratic weather patterns cause either water wastage or crop drought stress, while improper chemical fertilizer application depletes soil organic carbon over time.

**Krishi Saathi resolves these bottlenecks** by putting an AI-guided agronomist, livestock veterinarian advisor, market linkage engine, and learning academy into every farmer's pocket.

## 3. Key Features

- **Botanist-Grade Crop Identification & Diagnosis**: Uses computer vision through the Gemini API to identify species, analyze leaf morphology, detect pathogens, and calculate severity ratings with actionable organic and chemical remediation steps.
- **Comprehensive Livestock & Aquaculture Academy**: Structured courses, embedded video tutorials, interactive quizzes, and specialized feed/gestation calculators across 8 farming disciplines.
- **Hyper-Local Weather & Sowing Advisory**: Location-aware temperature, humidity, wind velocity, precipitation forecasts, and proactive heatwave/storm mitigation alerts.
- **Smart Nutrient & Irrigation Planners**: Tailored N-P-K nutrient schedules, organic manure recommendations, and drip/sprinkler water scheduling based on growth stage and field size.
- **Government Agricultural Scheme Matching**: Natural language query engine matching farmers with state and central subsidies (PM-KISAN, PMFBY crop insurance, Soil Health Card, KCC, micro-irrigation grants).
- **Direct Farm Marketplace & Negotiation**: Farmers list harvests directly with target price per quintal/kg, while buyers purchase verified farm produce and agricultural inputs with order tracking and in-app negotiation messaging.
- **Offline Field Guides**: Emergency agronomy and livestock first-aid field guides cached directly in the browser for use in rural areas with poor connectivity.
- **Role-Based Workspaces**: Tailored interfaces for Farmers, Commercial Sellers, Buyers, and Platform Administrators.

## 4. Crop Farming

The Crop Farming module provides comprehensive agronomic support for major cereal, cash, pulse, and horticulture crops (Wheat, Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane, Maize, Pulses, Mustard, and Tomato).

- **Species & Soil Profiling**: Analyzes soil texture, ideal pH ranges (5.5–8.0), native climate zones, and growth habit compatibility.
- **Stage-by-Stage Field Timeline**: Guides farmers from seedbed preparation, seed treatment, and spacing to vegetative weeding, critical flowering irrigation, ripening, and post-harvest storage.
- **Visual Pest & Disease Detector**: Dual-image input support allowing farmers to upload leaf symptoms alongside soil close-ups for comprehensive pathology reports.
- **Organic Remediation & Bio-Pesticides**: Formulates neem oil spray schedules, trichoderma seed treatments, and vermicompost application rates to reduce dependency on costly synthetic inputs.
- **Chemical Treatment Guidelines**: Specifies recommended active ingredients, precise dilution ratios, waiting periods before harvest, and personal protective precautions.

## 5. Animal & Fish Farming

Krishi Saathi provides an in-depth animal husbandry and aquatic farming curriculum organized into 8 distinct disciplines:

### Dairy Farming
- Breeds covered: Murrah Buffalo, Sahiwal, Gir, Red Sindhi, Holstein-Friesian crossbreeds.
- Key modules: Clean milk production protocols, silage preparation and green fodder planning, mastitis detection and CMT testing, artificial insemination timings, and calf rearing.

### Sheep Farming
- Breeds covered: Deccani, Nellore, Marwari, Bellary, and Merino crosses.
- Key modules: Semi-intensive and extensive pasture management, seasonal shearing cycles, wool grading, foot rot prevention, internal parasite deworming calendars, and lamb fattening.

### Goat Farming
- Breeds covered: Jamunapari, Sirohi, Black Bengal, Boer, and Osmanabadi.
- Key modules: Elevated stall-fed housing systems, browsing forage management, kidding care, enterotoxemia (ET) vaccination schedules, and commercial goat meat/milk economics.

### Poultry Farming
- Systems covered: Commercial Broilers, Egg Layers, and Free-Range Desi / Kadaknath country fowl.
- Key modules: Day-old chick brooding temperature curves, litter management and ammonia control, biosecurity barriers, Newcastle (Ranikhet) disease and Gumboro vaccination, and feed conversion ratio (FCR) calculation.

### Pig Farming
- Breeds covered: Large White Yorkshire, Landrace, and indigenous upgraded crosses.
- Key modules: Sow gestation management, farrowing pen preparation to prevent crushing, iron injections for piglets, hygienic swill and commercial feed formulation, and African Swine Fever biosecurity.

### Beekeeping (Apiculture)
- Species covered: *Apis mellifera* (European honeybee) and *Apis cerana indica* (Indian honeybee).
- Key modules: Modern Langstroth hive setup, seasonal floral calendar mapping, swarm prevention, queen bee rearing, varroa mite non-chemical management, hygienic honey extraction, and beeswax processing.

### Fisheries & Aquaculture
- Species covered: Indian Major Carps (Catla, Rohu, Mrigal), exotic carps, and Pangasius catfish.
- Key modules: Earthen pond excavation and liming, water quality testing (dissolved oxygen, pH 7.5–8.5, ammonia toxicity), fingerling stocking density, supplementary pellet feeding, paddlewheel aeration, and netting/harvest protocols.

### Integrated Farming Systems (IFS)
- Models covered: Rice-Fish-Azolla cultivation, Dairy-Biogas-Slurry-Crop circular loops, and Duck-Fish-Pond integration.
- Key modules: Nutrient recycling where animal waste fertilizes crops and ponds, farm biomass self-sufficiency, multi-revenue stream stabilization, and zero-waste farming methodologies.

## 6. Farming Academy

The Farming Academy serves as an interactive vocational learning center for rural practitioners:

- **Modular Guides**: Curriculum-based guides broken into bite-sized chapters with clear learning objectives, equipment checklists, and seasonal calendars.
- **Embedded Video Lessons**: Curated, high-quality video demonstrations embedded directly inside lesson views illustrating real farm techniques (such as mastitis screening, hive inspection, fish pond liming, and artificial brooding).
- **Learning Progress Tracking**: Individual farmer progress is persistently recorded with completed lesson markers, category completion percentages, and milestone achievement indicators.
- **Interactive Knowledge Quizzes**: End-of-module assessment quizzes testing key agronomic concepts (e.g., feed ratios, vaccine timings, water pH thresholds) with instant answer verification, score tallies, and explanatory feedback.
- **Specialized Agricultural Calculators**:
  - *Dairy Feed & Dry Matter Calculator*: Calculates daily green fodder, dry roughage, and concentrate requirements based on body weight and milk yield.
  - *Dairy Gestation & Calving Timeline*: Projects drying-off dates and expected calving windows from service date.
  - *Poultry FCR & Batch Profitability*: Computes Feed Conversion Ratio and margins per bird.
  - *Aquaculture Stocking & Pellet Estimator*: Calculates fingerling stocking rates and daily feed dosage based on pond water volume.

## 7. Bilingual Language System

Language accessibility is a core architectural pillar of Krishi Saathi. Agricultural terminology is presented so that farmers can comfortably read instructions in their regional mother tongue while retaining standard technical and botanical references in English.

### Supported Languages
1. **English (`en`)**: Base default language across all schemas, APIs, and botanical names.
2. **Hindi (`hi`)**: Comprehensive translations across UI controls, diagnostics, marketplace, and guides.
3. **Marathi (`mr`)**: Full regional translations tailored to Maharashtra's farming communities.
4. **Maithili (`mai`)**: Dedicated regional script and vocabulary for Bihar and eastern agricultural belts.
5. **Bhojpuri (`bho`)**: Dialect-specific agricultural translations for eastern Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
6. **Additional Regional & International Locales**: Bengali (`bn`), Telugu (`te`), Tamil (`ta`), Punjabi (`pa`), Spanish (`es`), Vietnamese (`vi`), and Swahili (`sw`).

### English + Selected Language Display
The application uses a custom dual-text rendering pattern (`Bilingual` component) across critical headings, action buttons, table labels, and alert banners:
- **Primary Line**: Displays the English term (ensuring universal technical consistency).
- **Secondary Line / Subtitle**: Displays the corresponding regional translation (Hindi, Marathi, Maithili, Bhojpuri, etc.) directly alongside or underneath.
- **Dynamic Context Switching**: Farmers can switch languages on the fly from the persistent header selector without losing form state or resetting active camera captures.

## 8. GPS-Based Farm Location

- **Browser Geolocation Integration**: Farmers can click the "Detect Farm GPS" button during registration or from the weather module, triggering `navigator.geolocation.getCurrentPosition()`.
- **Heuristic Reverse-Geocoding**: Maps coordinates to Indian states and agro-climatic districts (e.g., Maharashtra/Pune, Punjab/Ludhiana, Tamil Nadu/Coimbatore, Andhra Pradesh/Guntur, Bihar/Patna).
- **Auto-Locale Adaptation**: When GPS detects a specific region, the system automatically suggests the relevant primary regional language (e.g., Punjabi for Punjab coordinates, Marathi for Maharashtra).
- **Precision Agronomy Anchoring**: Coordinates associate diagnostic records, weather predictions, and marketplace listings with the farmer's physical territory.

## 10. Weather

The weather service combines real-time meteorological parameters with generative agricultural intelligence:

- **Live Meteorological Tracking**: Reports ambient temperature (°C), relative humidity (%), surface wind speed (km/h), and precipitation likelihood for the farmer's district.
- **Proactive Risk Warnings**: Automatically flags extreme weather events such as sudden temperature spikes (heatwaves), unseasonal rain, frost risk, or high humidity conducive to fungal blights.
- **AI-Driven Seasonal Farming Recommendations**: Sends the microclimate profile to Gemini to generate actionable agronomic advice:
  - Immediate field actions (e.g., apply mulch to reduce evapotranspiration; postpone pesticide spraying before expected showers).
  - Recommended crop varieties suited to the upcoming seasonal forecast.
  - Optimal sowing windows and estimated days to maturity.

## 11. AI/Gemini Features

Krishi Saathi leverages Google DeepMind's `@google/genai` SDK on the server side (`server.ts` and `src/server/gemini.ts`) using the `gemini-2.5-flash` model:

| AI Feature | Backend Function | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Plant Identification** | `identifyPlant()` | Multimodal image analysis returning scientific name, common names, family, growth habit, and soil compatibility assessment. |
| **Disease Detection** | `detectDisease()` | Identifies fungal, bacterial, viral, or deficiency symptoms; outputs severity score, organic remedies, and chemical fungicides/pesticides. |
| **Crop Recommendations** | `recommendCrops()` | Recommends optimal crops given state, district, soil type, and season, with detailed sowing and harvest calendars. |
| **Fertilizer Planning** | `planFertilizer()` | Calculates basal and top-dressing N-P-K nutrient schedules, organic manure volumes, and micronutrient timings for specific field acreages. |
| **Irrigation Scheduling** | `planIrrigation()` | Designs watering intervals, critical growth stage moisture requirements, and suitable irrigation methods (drip/sprinkler/basin). |
| **Government Schemes** | `queryGovernmentSchemes()` | Natural language query interface matching farmer eligibility against central and state agricultural subsidy programs. |
| **Farming Assistant Chat** | `chatFarmingAssistant()` | Multi-turn agricultural chatbot answering conversational voice and text inquiries in the farmer's chosen regional language. |
| **Yield Forecasting** | `forecastYield()` | Predictive modeling calculating expected harvest yield per acre, market revenue projections, and risk mitigation strategies. |
| **Voice Audio Transcription** | `transcribeAudio()` | Converts spoken regional voice notes (base64 audio) into text for hands-free query processing. |

All Gemini API calls are strictly executed server-side to safeguard API credentials and ensure reliable response handling with structured JSON extraction and fallback defaults.

## 12. Marketplace

The marketplace links rural producers directly with retail consumers, bulk buyers, restaurants, and agricultural input distributors:

- **Harvest Marketplace (Crop Listings)**:
  - Farmers list harvested or standing crops with photos, variety name, quantity available (quintals/kg), minimum order quantity, harvest date, and asking price.
  - Quality certifications and verification badges (e.g., Organic Certified, Grade A, Lab Tested).
- **Agricultural Inputs Catalog**:
  - Seeds (cereals, pulses, vegetables, hybrid seeds).
  - Organic and bio-fertilizers (vermicompost, neem cake, bio-NPK).
  - Crop protection inputs (organic bio-pesticides, pheromone traps).
  - Farm machinery and equipment (rotavators, seed drills, sprayers, solar pumps, drip kits).
- **Negotiation & In-App Chat**: Direct real-time messaging between buyers and farmers to discuss bulk pricing, packaging specifications, and delivery logistics.
- **Order Management & Checkout**:
  - Itemized shopping cart and simulated checkout flow supporting UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
  - Digital invoice/receipt generation with order tracking from Pending to Confirmed, In-Transit, and Delivered.
- **Seller & Buyer Dashboards**: Dedicated order status oversight, inventory management, revenue metrics, and transaction histories.

## 13. User Roles

The platform enforces role-based workflows and access control (`src/types.ts`):

1. **Farmer (`farmer`)**:
   - Primary user focused on crop and livestock health.
   - Access to diagnosis tools, planners, academy courses, weather advisory, government schemes, and crop listing creation.
2. **Seller (`seller`)**:
   - Agricultural merchant or input dealer.
   - Manages product inventory (seeds, fertilizers, tools), processes orders, tracks buyer communications, and reviews sales revenue.
3. **Buyer (`buyer`)**:
   - Grain trader, food processor, retail consumer, or cooperative purchaser.
   - Searches verified farm produce, compares market rates, initiates negotiations, places orders, and tracks fulfillment.
4. **Admin (`admin`)**:
   - Agricultural extension supervisor or system administrator.
   - Verifies farmer and seller credentials, moderates listings, removes flagged content, monitors system health, and accesses database export utilities.

## 18. Local Installation

Follow these steps to run Krishi Saathi locally on your workstation:

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js**: Version 18.0.0 or later (Node 20+ recommended)
- **Package Manager**: `npm` (included with Node.js) or `bun`
- **Gemini API Key**: Obtain a key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/)

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/namit27y/krishi-saathi.git
   cd krishi-saathi
   ```

2. **Install project dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Configure environment variables**:
   ```bash
   cp .env.example .env
   ```
   Open `.env` and supply your actual `GEMINI_API_KEY`.

4. **Start the development server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

5. **Access the application**:
   Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```

## 21. Gemini API Setup

Krishi Saathi requires the Gemini API for botanist-grade plant identification, disease diagnosis, weather recommendation, and chat.

## 22. Backend & API Overview

The backend is an Express application hosted directly on port 3000 (`server.ts`), serving both the REST endpoints and the client-side SPA bundle.

- `POST /api/auth/register`: Create a new user with role, location, mobile, and soil attributes.
- `POST /api/auth/login`: Authenticate existing user by email/password.
- `GET /api/auth/profile`: Retrieve profile information for the authenticated user.
- `PUT /api/auth/profile/update`: Update farmer land size, soil type, and location details.

#### AI Diagnostics & Agronomy
- `POST /api/plant-id/analyze`: Multimodal plant identification and soil compatibility.
- `GET /api/plant-id/logs`: Retrieve historical plant identification logs.
- `POST /api/disease/analyze`: Crop leaf disease detection with remediation prescriptions.
- `GET /api/disease/reports`: History of diagnosed crop pathology reports.
- `POST /api/plans/fertilizer`: Nutrient calculation (NPK and organic fertilizers).
- `POST /api/plans/irrigation`: Irrigation schedule and method recommendations.
- `POST /api/gemini/yield-forecast`: Predictive harvest yield and revenue model.

#### Weather & Advisory
- `GET /api/weather/current`: Real-time temperature, humidity, wind, and alerts for district.
- `POST /api/weather/recommend`: Gemini-powered agronomic insights tailored to the microclimate.

#### Farming Assistant & Voice
- `POST /api/chat`: Multi-turn conversational agronomic assistant.
- `GET /api/chat/history`: Previous interaction history for authenticated user.
- `POST /api/audio/transcribe`: Audio transcription for regional voice queries.

#### Marketplace & Negotiation
- `GET /api/marketplace/crops`: Fetch available farmer crop listings with optional filtering.
- `POST /api/marketplace/crops`: Create a new harvest listing with images and pricing.
- `PUT /api/marketplace/crops/:id`: Update existing crop listing.
- `DELETE /api/marketplace/crops/:id`: Remove crop listing.
- `GET /api/marketplace/products`: Fetch commercial agricultural inputs (seeds, tools, inputs).

- `GET /api/marketplace/orders`: Retrieve user purchase or sale orders.

## 23. Database Architecture

### Active Engine: JSON File Persistence (`data/db.json`)
The application currently operates on a fast, zero-configuration embedded persistence engine (`src/server/db.ts`):
- Data is stored in `data/db.json` and mirrored in memory for sub-millisecond query responses.
- Write operations trigger asynchronous atomic filesystem flushes to prevent corruption.
- Initialized with realistic agricultural seed datasets (hybrid seeds, bio-fertilizers, solar sprayers, tractor attachments).

### Planned Relational Engine: MySQL 8.x
A fully normalized SQL schema has been designed and is exported directly by the application at `GET /api/db/create-script`:

- `Users`: Farmer/seller credentials, geolocation, land size, soil type, preferred language.
- `Plant_Species`: Botanical taxonomy, scientific names, ideal pH ranges, water and sun requirements.
- `Plant_Local_Names`: Regional dialect names for plants across Indian states.
- `Plant_Diseases_Reference`: Pathogen database, symptom signatures, organic and chemical controls.
- `Plant_Identification_Logs`: User scans, confidence scores, and soil compatibility ratings.
- `Disease_Reports`: Specific leaf diagnosis incidents with image URLs and recovery forecasts.
- `Crop_Listings`: Farmer harvest offerings, quantities, asking prices, and verification status.
- `Agricultural_Products`: Commercial catalog items, inventory counts, categories, and ratings.
- `Marketplace_Orders`: Order line items, buyer/seller references, and delivery stages.
- `Payments` & `Transactions`: Payment gateway reference IDs, payment methods, and timestamps.
- `Marketplace_Chats`: Real-time negotiation logs between buyers and sellers.
- `Chat_History`: Conversational memory with the Gemini agricultural assistant.
- `Notifications`: System alerts, weather warnings, and order updates.

*Migration to native MySQL with connection pooling and migrations is actively in development.*

## 24. Security Considerations

- **Server-Side API Key Isolation**: The `GEMINI_API_KEY` is strictly accessed within server-side Node.js code (`process.env.GEMINI_API_KEY`). Keys are never compiled into or exposed through client-side JavaScript bundles.
- **Input Validation & Sanitization**: Incoming request payloads (crops, orders, user profiles) are validated before storage to prevent malformed data injection.
- **Role Enforcement**: Critical administrative endpoints (`/api/admin/*`) require verification of the requesting user's role before processing.
- **Image Payload Limits**: Image data uploaded for leaf and plant inspection is processed with size constraints and clean MIME-type extraction to mitigate Denial of Service (DoS) memory pressure.
- **Safe Fallback Design**: In the event of network timeouts or third-party service degradation, the server utilizes built-in fallback routines so that farmers are not left stranded without basic advice.
